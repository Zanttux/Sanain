# AI development policy
Claude is the primary operational developer and maintainer under human leadership. Aim for AI-authored project-owned implementation while welcoming human contributions. Disclose actual provenance and exceptions, including human patches and third-party code. Never imply dependencies were AI-written. Human reviews architecture, tests, security and releases. AI output is untrusted until reviewed; verify licenses and provenance, avoid copying incompatible code. Record model/tool, task, date, review and test evidence without secrets or private prompts. No user files sent to external models by default. Runtime AI is separate, optional, post-v1 and must show destination, payload preview and consent; local endpoint preferred. Never execute AI output automatically. Copyrightability of generated code varies by jurisdiction; obtain advice before making legal claims.
