# Governance
The founder is accountable and leads Sanain. Claude is the primary operational developer and maintainer, not a legal owner or independent human reviewer. Community feedback and human contributions are welcome. Decisions follow docs/DECISIONS.md and docs/AUTONOMY.md. Sponsors have no special merge rights. New maintainers require owner approval, least privilege and 2FA. Owner retains recovery access. No fabricated consensus, approvals or AI provenance. Product quality is the objective; no separate research program.
