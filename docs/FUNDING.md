# Funding and governance
All features free for everyone including companies. No paywall, ads, telemetry, mandatory account or sponsor influence over technical decisions. Primary channels: GitHub Sponsors, one-time donations, corporate sponsorship. Optional identical store distribution at $9.99 only after human approval and store/license review. Free official downloads remain available. Suggested tiers $3/$10 individual, $49/$149/$499 company; not commitments. Sponsor recognition on website/README with consent; no in-editor ads. Publish periodic aggregate income and costs without exposing private donor data. Verify Finnish tax/accounting and VAT treatment with professional advice before accepting funds; distinguish donations from sales/services. No fundraising account created by agents.

Scenario: 100,000 new users/year, 2% buy $9.99 store package = $19,980 gross; 100 recurring $5 supporters = $6,000/year; five $149/month sponsors = $8,940/year; total $34,920 before fees/taxes/costs. Hypothetical, not forecast. Track actual costs and opt-in feedback, not user telemetry. Budget for signing, hosting, CI, AI usage and support. No promised sponsor benefits that compromise independence.
